My Journey of Becoming a Web Developer

My Journey of Becoming a Web Developer

2020 - 2022

Started learning HTML & CSS

In the summer of 2020, just as I was starting high school, I discovered the world of HTML & CSS. Fascinated by the idea of creating websites from scratch, I dived into countless tutorials and online courses. Each line of code felt like a new tool in my creative toolbox, allowing me to bring my ideas to life on the screen. Slowly but surely, web design became more than just a hobby, it became a way for me to express myself digitally.

2023 - 2024

Upgrading with Javascript & PHP

By 2023, I started diving deeper into Javascript & PHP, exploring new ways to make my projects more dynamic. Each small experiment taught me something new, and I could see my skills improving with every line of code. It felt like I was truly leveling up as a developer.

2024 - 2026

Falling in Love with Webflow & Figma

By 2024, I was diving deeper into web development, and my journey had reached an exciting stage. I spent time honing my skills with HTML, building solid structures that formed the backbone of my projects. CSS allowed me to experiment with layouts and styles, making my creations visually engaging. Then I explored JavaScript, adding interactivity and dynamic features that made my websites come alive. PHP opened new possibilities for server-side logic, letting me manage data and create more complex functionality. All of these experiences came together as I started working with Webflow & Figma, designing and prototyping ina way that felt both creative and powerful
